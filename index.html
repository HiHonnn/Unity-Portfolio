01 / ECHOES INSIDE▶WATCH GAMEPLAY

2D PUZZLE ADVENTURE / ACADEMIC PROJECT

Echoes Inside

Echoes Inside is a 2D puzzle adventure about a family struggling to get along. Each family member has their own story and struggles. You play as a child in the family, exploring each room and solving puzzles to understand what the others are going through.

The demo includes three chapters. Each introduces a different way to play and offers a new perspective on the tensions within the family.

  • 2D player movement with Rigidbody2D and directional animations with Animator.
  • Room-specific camera boundaries using Cinemachine Confiner 2D.
  • Scene transitions using Unity’s SceneManager, with inventory and chapter progress preserved during play.
C#Cinemachine2D PhysicsDesign Patterns

A closer look at the code +

Developed for a design patterns course. A persistent GameManager tracks chapter progress; C# events connect puzzle completion to game flow. The project is also where I practise separating gameplay rules from scene and UI logic.

3D SPACE SHOOTER / GUIDED PROJECT

Galaxy Strike

Fly, aim and fight your way through enemy waves. A spaceship shooter that brings movement, particle weapons and cinematic sequences together in one playable level.

  • Bounded flight controls with smooth pitch and roll.
  • Mouse-aimed weapons, enemy health and score tracking.
  • Enemy waves, dialogue and voice lines using Timeline & Signals.
